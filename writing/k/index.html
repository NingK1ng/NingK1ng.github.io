k k

November 2025

For quite a long time, I stopped writing anything. I don’t know why. One day, while I was on the toilet, I spent ten minutes writing this piece of flash fiction in a note—I don’t know why either.

相当长一段时间我不再创作任何文字，我不知道原因。在某天如厕时，我花了10分钟在便签里写下这篇微小说——我也不知道原因。

10 years later, k knocked on my door, still looking just as soaking wet. To be precise, as I was entering my apartment, k rushed up from the darkness in the corridor and held the door open with his foot. He said: Hello there, it’s been 10 years.

I didn’t react, just went on taking off my shoes in the entryway. I could hear k dripping away at the door, wetting my rug. The apartment’s flooring was all solid wood, which would swell when wet.

k came in anyway, without even taking off his shoes. 10 years ago, I had pushed him into a reservoir, where he drowned. His floating corpse drifted for days before washing ashore. When k’s family came to identify the body, they stared intently for more than 10 minutes before finally breaking into loud sobs. k was thinking then: 20 years spent with me, and it took only a few days for them not to recognize me.

Water ghosts suffer not only as they drown.

For the next two years, k contacted me often. He talked about how things were in life, how things were in death; I neither agreed nor disagreed. This tiresome small talk was a minor matter. I was long used to it; the trouble was the other questions he tried to lead into. Reappearing after 8 years away, k had not changed at all, except that he looked even more like a water ghost—he must have regretted the long hair he’d had when he died. For a while, k had visited often, and I’d specifically kept a dry mop ready to prevent the floor from swelling. Whenever k saw the mop, he would make fun of his own hair: If I’d known, I wouldn’t have grown this mop.

k spoke again, interrupting my recollections and my silence. I was like a hard-coded program: whatever he said, I would ask him the same question in return: Do you remember your promise? That was why he had disappeared for 8 years. After 8 years, he seemed to think I would have forgotten, and had come back to bother me. k was like a program too. After my question, it was as though he activated the personality he’d had in life, then decayed over time back to his normal state. I waited patiently for his reaction to end, then said: k, you’ve forgotten—my solid wood floor isn’t waterproof. k left. He said: Next time I’ll wear a spacesuit to your place. The distinctive humor of water ghosts is on a par with the pain they endure.

The coffee was steaming, yet it still reflected my face. Back then, I had made him die 57 years early. In 47 years, he will die without illness. It’s been 10 years. He should have finished watching his original life by now, I suppose.

I leaned back in my chair, waited for the coffee to cool a little, then began my midnight work. Only when I remembered the water stains k had left behind did I reluctantly get up to clean them. People all call me the Grim Reaper. Who would have thought all I do is wipe other people’s asses like this: help k die or mop up the water k has spilled.

One more thing: k’s promise was to forget the girl who had caused his death. It’s been 10 years, and he still hasn’t forgotten. Water ghosts have their own difficulties, and he keeps coming to me with questions; he must have seen that he would originally have spent his life with her, I suppose.

10年后k敲响了我的门，还是那副湿漉漉的样子。确切的说是我进公寓时，楼道暗处的k冲上来拿脚抵住了门。他说：你好啊，10年了。

我没有反应，自顾自地在玄关脱鞋。我听见k在门口一直滴水，弄湿了我的地毯。公寓地板全是实木的，遇水会发泡。

k还是进来了，甚至没脱鞋。10年前他被我推下水库溺毙，浮尸漂泊数天上岸，k的家人认尸时聚精会神看了十分钟有余，方才嚎啕大哭。k那时候在想：与我同处20年，辨认不出只需数天。

水鬼，不仅溺毙时痛苦。

那以后的两年，k经常与我联系。说活着怎么怎么样，死了怎么怎么样，我不置可否。这些碎嘴家常是小事，我早已听惯，难缠的是他试图引出的其他问题。消失8年现身，k除了更像一只水鬼，没有任何变化——他肯定后悔死时蓄的长发。有段时间k经常来访，我特意准备了个干拖把避免地板发泡，k看到拖把就会拿自己头发自嘲：早知不留这拖把头了。

k又说话了，打断我的回忆与沉默。我像一个写死的程序，不管他说什么，我都反问一句：你记得你的承诺吗？这就是他消失8年的原因。8年后他好像觉得我会忘记，又跑来烦我。k也像个程序，我反问后，他就好像激活了活着的那套性格，然后再随时间衰减到正常状态。我耐心等他反应结束，说：k，你忘啦，我的实木地板不防水。k走了。他说：下次我穿宇航服来你这。水鬼独特的幽默，与它们承受的痛苦相当。

咖啡冒着热气，却仍映着我的脸。当初我让他早死了57年。47年后，他会无疾而终。10年了，他应该看完自己原来的一生了吧。

我靠着椅背，等咖啡凉些，便着手午夜工作。又想起k留下来的水渍，才不情愿地起身收拾。人们都叫我死神，谁晓得我做的都是这种给人擦屁股的事：帮k去死或者把k洒下的水渍用拖把拖干净。

还有，k的承诺是忘掉那个让他死的女孩。10年了，还没忘掉，水鬼有自己的难处，老找我打听；应该是看到自己本来与她共度一生了吧。
