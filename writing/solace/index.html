Solace | 慰

July 18, 2026

The autumn wind consoles the fallen leaves, blurred and wavering, again displaced

When spring grows warm, the yellow has left you My solace turns into soil, earthworms, humus

Again and again I search for a soul once held dear

—green, poetry, amnesia I let the treetops utter a few muted, husky sounds, in memory of you and me, and the touch of growing old

秋风抚慰落叶 影影绰绰，又 流离失所

暖春时， 你不再泛黄 我的慰藉，化为 泥土、蚯蚓、腐殖质

反复寻找一个，曾被 在意的魂灵

——绿色 诗意，失忆 我让树梢发出 些许暗哑声， 纪念你我，以及 衰老的触感
