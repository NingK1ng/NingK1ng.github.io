Shaving | 剃须

December 23, 2025

The smell of childhood, over and over In sunlight, dust like weeds bursts into bloom, then falls Your shirtfront— when did it cease to be wet with tears? And the sounds of companions?

—they are an unassuming sheet of paper, crumpled into a ball or torn apart The traces in the paper have long since become, through folds once neatly made, a paper crane, spreading wings, soaring high, nestling against your arm again

Sleep, then wake Outside, by the window— should have been woken by playmates A gentle stroke across the diary, words sprouting like stubble, sparse, dishevelled, unwashed— childhood friends or passersby. The razor wipes all away; yet stubble sprouts again, unbidden

童年的气味反反复复 阳光里尘埃如野草 怒放然后坠落 你的衣襟 何时不再沾满涕泪 还有， 同伴的声响？

——他们是淡泊的一张纸 揉搓成一团亦或撕毁 纸中的痕迹，早已 成为某次折叠规整的 千纸鹤 展翅高飞 再依偎你的臂膀

睡一觉然后醒来 屋外窗前 本该被玩伴叫醒 轻抚日记本， 长出胡茬般的文字 稀稀疏疏、蓬头垢面 发小亦或过客， 剃须刀全都抹净 又擅自冒出青茬
