Attractor | 吸引子

June 27, 2025

Years upon years, months upon months, coalesce into a single attractor The sun’s membrane potential, there, rises and falls

The manifold of time falls in a fine drizzle, covering both my hands— and so I become the sum of it

许许多多年年月月 汇聚成一粒 吸引子 太阳的膜电位，在那里 起起落落

时间的流形 淅淅沥沥，覆盖我的双手 ——我于是成为 它的总和
