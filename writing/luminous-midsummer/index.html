Luminous Midsummer | 朗朗盛夏

August 9, 2026

Every midsummer night, to stars on the verge of dying, I speak of the daylight soon to come— what shape it will take

Is sunrise a kind of feeling? Leaves sip at dew Wind sounds through the woods, in concert with cicadas. In the halos of the creek, I see myself nearing the coffin

—This feeling lies across my neck. In every tomorrow murmuring in its sleep, shadows bleed into the scattered night; my absence flows into luminous midsummer

Green grasses cling to the muddy universe The coming day, the next season, the next epoch— a stone, or a cow?
