Listening | 听

April 24, 2026

How many grains of time come and go

Listen to the raindrops striking its gold

The seen and the unseen are listening

Once again I remember that damp childhood, dancing in the rain, its arms scattered with sounds steeped in earth

来来去去多少粒 时间

听雨点击打它的 金黄

那些可见的和不可见的 都在聆听

我复又想起 潮湿的童年，它在 雨水里舞蹈 臂膀洒满 沁着泥土的 音
