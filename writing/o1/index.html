O1

December 23, 2024
Image placeholder

My O1 visa was approved today.

I moved from India to the US a little over 8 years ago. I’ve been moving around my entire life, but this was the only place that inspired me to build something of my own.

In hindsight, being an immigrant was a blessing in disguise. Not having the same opportunities as everyone else meant I could spend my free time building and doing things I actually cared about.

This year, I went all in. I turned down multiple job offers to continue working on my startups full time, and getting this visa was the only way I could sustain it long term.

Thank you Sam Altman and Garry Tan for the recommendation letters, Sigil for helping me throughout, Minn Kim and Lighthouse for making it easy, and everyone else for believing in me.
